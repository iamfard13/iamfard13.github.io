## 📖 Introduction

This is a short article about microservices architecture.

### Key Points

- **Decentralized**: Each service runs independently.
- **Scalable**: Services can be scaled individually.
- **Resilient**: Failure in one service doesn't bring down the whole system.

### Why Microservices?

They allow teams to develop, deploy, and maintain services independently, which speeds up development and improves flexibility.
